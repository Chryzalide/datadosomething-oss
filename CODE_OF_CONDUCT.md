# Code of conduct

This project is small and the standard is ordinary: be decent to people.

Concretely — take part in a way that would be fine to read back a year later.
Assume the person on the other end is doing their best with less context than you
have. Criticise the code, the reasoning and the decision, not the person who
wrote them. Harassment, personal attacks, and abuse in any form are not welcome
here, in issues, pull requests, commit messages or anywhere else the project
happens.

If something crosses that line, or you are not sure whether it did, write to
**datadosomething.doctrine508@passmail.net**. Reports go to the maintainer and
nobody else. You will get a reply, and a report will never be dismissed for being
about something small.

The maintainer may edit or remove contributions that do not meet this standard,
and may block accounts, with an explanation given where that is possible.

This is deliberately short rather than adopted wholesale from a template: a
document nobody read is not a commitment. If the project ever grows past one
maintainer it will want the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org)
and a named enforcement process, and this should be replaced rather than
extended.
